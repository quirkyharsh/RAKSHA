About RAKSHA


RAKSHA Mission

RAKSHA is a personal safety platform designed to ensure that people can seek immediate help during emergencies. Our mission is to empower individuals by providing them with a quick and efficient way to notify their loved ones or emergency contacts in moments of distress.

Safety Check

When you register yourself by clicking on the SAFETY MODE you will get a call after every five minutes asking for your safety.

User Simplicity

The system is built with simplicity in mind, ensuring that users can quickly press a button and notify their emergency contacts when needed. RAKSHA provides peace of mind, knowing that help is just a click away.
