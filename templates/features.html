Press 1: Safety Confirmation

Confirms that you are safe. The system will thank you, and further calls will be made after wait period of five minutes to allow you to reassess your safety.

Press 9: Emergency Alert

Indicates that you are in danger. Immediate alerts are sent to your emergency contacts, notifying them that you need help urgently.

Press 3: Stop Follow-Up

Marks yourself as safe and stops any further follow-up. No additional calls or notifications will be sent after pressing this option.
